Privacy Policy

How BCAHub collects, uses, and protects your information. We believe in full transparency.

Last updated: Aug 05, 2026 Applies to: pixels111.github.io/bcahub and all subdomains Version 4.0 English
Our Privacy Promise
BCAHub is a free, student-built platform. We do not sell your data, show targeted ads, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes. Your privacy is genuinely important to us.
01

Introduction

Welcome to BCAHub ("we", "us", "our"). BCAHub is a free academic resource platform built by and for BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) students. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

By accessing or using BCAHub (available at pixels111.github.io/bcahub), you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our website.

This policy applies to all visitors, users, and anyone who interacts with BCAHub including submitting reviews, using our tools, downloading resources, or filling out any forms on the site.

02

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate and improve BCAHub.

Information you voluntarily provide
When you submit a review, contact form, or feedback, you may provide your name, email address, and written comments. This is entirely optional and voluntary.
  • Name: Collected when you submit a review. Displayed publicly if you approve it.
  • Email Address: Collected only if you opt-in to notifications. Never displayed publicly.
  • Contact Number: Collected only if you opt-in to notifications. Never displayed publicly.
  • Feedback / Comments: Your written review content. Published only with your explicit approval.
  • Content Galaxy / Resource Request Forms: Messages, resource requests, links, and files (if sent via email).
We do NOT collect
We do not collect personal data such as IP addresses or cookies. GitHub may log basic usage statistics in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Policy .

Resource Cache: To improve loading performance, BCAHub temporarily stores publicly available resource metadata and file information in your browser's session storage. This cache contains only educational resource data, does not include personal information, is automatically refreshed when updated resources become available, and is cleared when your browser session ends.
03

How Forms Work

All form submissions are sent directly to a connected Database using Google Apps Script. This means no third party form service stores your data.

04

Content Disclaimer

Articles, programs, and resources on this website may include:

  • Publicly available educational resources collected from the internet (credited wherever possible).
  • Original content created by BCAHub, including notes, articles, programs, and study materials.
  • Contributions submitted by students and educators with their permission or appropriate attribution.

BCAHub is an open educational platform created to help students learn and share knowledge. We provide original study materials created by BCAHub, along with educational resources collected from publicly available sources.

BCAHub original content may be copied, shared, uploaded, or republished on other websites, apps, or platforms. We kindly request that proper credit be given to BCAHub. Alternatively, you may use our Copy Ref-Link feature to link directly to the original resource on BCAHub, allowing your users to redirect visitors from their platform to the original source while supporting proper attribution.

Resources that are not originally created by BCAHub remain subject to their respective owners' rights and licenses. Their reuse, distribution, or publication should comply with the original source's terms and applicable copyright laws.

05

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries: We use your contact details to reply to your message.
  • To improve our content based on feedback: Your comments and suggestions help us enhance the resources available.
  • To make resources up to date: We regularly update our materials to ensure they remain current and relevant.
  • To add new content you suggest: We welcome your ideas for additional resources that could benefit the community.

We do not use your data for advertising, tracking, or third-party sales.

Performance Optimization
To provide faster loading times and reduce unnecessary network requests, BCAHub temporarily caches publicly available resource information within your browser's session storage. This cache is used solely to improve website performance and does not contain personal information.
06

Third-Party Services

BCAHub integrates with several third-party services to deliver content and functionality. Each service has its own privacy policy and terms governing its data practices.

  • Google Apps Script: Used to securely retrieve resource metadata, file information, and Google Drive links from our connected Google Sheets database. No personal information is collected through these requests.
  • Google Drive: Used to host downloadable study resources such as notes, PDFs, and other educational materials. Governed by Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: BCAHub is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server logs in accordance with its Privacy Statement.
  • YouTube: Embedded videos (where available) may set cookies or collect usage information when you interact with the player.
  • Manabadi / TCS iON: External result portals linked from BCAHub. Their privacy practices are governed by their respective policies.

BCAHub is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites and services. We encourage users to review their respective privacy policies before interacting with them.

07

Data Storage & Security

BCAHub is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages and does not maintain user accounts or operate its own servers. Resource metadata is retrieved securely through Google Apps Script from our connected Google database, while downloadable files are hosted on Google Drive.

  • Information submitted through contact forms, reviews, or contribution forms is securely stored in our connected database only for the time necessary to process your request.
  • Google Apps Script is used only to securely deliver website data.
  • To improve performance, BCAHub temporarily stores publicly available resource metadata and file information in your browser's session storage. This cached data is used only to speed up resource loading, is automatically refreshed when updated resource versions become available, contains no personal information, and is automatically cleared when your browser session ends.
  • Although we use trusted platforms and reasonable security measures, no method of online transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.
08

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information submitted to BCAHub:

  • Right to correction: You can request correction of inaccurate information in your published review.
  • Right to deletion: You can request removal of your published review at any time by emailing us.
  • Right to withdraw consent: If you approved a review for public display, you may withdraw that approval by contacting us. The review will be unpublished within 48 hours.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at generalbca@gmail.com. We will respond within 5 business days.

09

Reviews & Ratings

BCAHub has a review system for students who have used our services. Reviews are subject to the following specific terms:

  • Reviews are manually verified before publication.
  • If you approved public display, your name and comment will be visible to all visitors.
  • Fake, duplicate, abusive, or spam reviews will be permanently deleted and the submitter may be blocked from future submissions.
  • BCAHub reserves the right to withhold or remove any review that violates our community guidelines.

Continued use of BCAHub after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
